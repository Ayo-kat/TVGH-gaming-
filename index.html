About Us

Welcome to TVGH

The Viral Game Club Hub is a gaming and entertainment destination built for players who want more than just a screen. Located in City Square, Rustenburg, TVGH brings friends, competitors and casual gamers together under one roof for an exciting, comfortable and unforgettable gaming experience.

Whether you are here for a quick solo session, a friendly rivalry with your crew, or a full afternoon of gaming, snooker and refreshments — TVGH is the place to be.

  • Competitive gaming
  • Social entertainment
  • Multiplayer experiences
  • Gaming with friends
  • Refreshments & snacks
  • Comfortable gaming environment
